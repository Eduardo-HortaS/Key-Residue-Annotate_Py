Test data for the integration tests. Files taken from public releases, reduced to what the tests need.

proteome.fasta
    UniProtKB entries Q32ZE1, A7K8Y4, A7K940 and A7K9F8. UniProt, CC BY 4.0.

Pfam-A.hmm
    Models PF20907, PF01570, PF01607, PF01541 and PF07460 from Pfam 37.0. CC0.

resources/PF*/
    Seed alignments and HMMs from Pfam 37.0 (CC0). Annotations, conservations and extra donors
    built from Pfam 37.0 and UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot 2026_01 (UniProt, CC BY 4.0).

resources/mappings/interpro_pfam_accession_mapping.tsv
    InterPro to Pfam accession mapping, rows for the five families. InterPro, CC0.

resources/mappings/go-basic.obo
    Gene Ontology go-basic.obo, release 2025-02-06, reduced to the terms used by these files and
    their ancestors. Gene Ontology Consortium, CC BY 4.0.

iprscan_by_sequence/
    InterProScan output for the four sequences.
